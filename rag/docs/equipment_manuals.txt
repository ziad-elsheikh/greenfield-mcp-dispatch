[DOC_ID: MAN_001]
Equipment: SPR-3001 (Sprayer)
Operating Guidelines:
For spray jobs carrying restricted chemicals (such as Glyphosate), maximum speed must not exceed 12 km/h.
Buffer zones: Minimum 15 meters from any canal or irrigation channel for restricted class chemicals.
Maintenance: Nozzle inspection required every 50 operating hours.

[DOC_ID: MAN_002]
Equipment: TRC-2001 (Tractor)
Operating Guidelines:
Optimal tilling speed is 8 km/h. Fuel consumption increases significantly above 2200 RPM.
Soil Conditions: Do not operate on fields with clay moisture exceeding 35% to prevent soil compaction.
